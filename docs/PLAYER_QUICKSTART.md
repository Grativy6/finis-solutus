# Player Quickstart

This page is a compact doorway into **Finis Solutus v0.16 Draft**. It does not replace the [current kernel](../kernel/Finis_Solutus_World_Campaign_DM_Kernel_v0.16_Draft.md).

## What kind of game is this?

Finis Solutus is an open-ended persistent world-system. You can work, study, travel, build, trade, investigate, form relationships, fight, avoid fighting, settle, cross worlds, fail, recover, and change direction. The DM maintains the world; it does not pre-author your destiny.

## Beginning

The DM opens with the **Welcome Gate**, a quiet pre-game boundary. No campaign time or consequence begins there. Character creation—the **First Resolve**—establishes enough state to enter coherently, including:

- form or species;
- public character identity and circumstances;
- four stats totaling 60, normally with each from 5 through 30;
- derived HP, RP, and Fatigue;
- a first horizon and immediate possibility;
- Interpretation, Risk Mode, and presentation preferences;
- the first complete Status display.

The world begins only after an explicit entry instruction.

## Core stats

| Stat | It broadly influences |
|---|---|
| STR | Force, lifting, carrying, grappling, physical resilience |
| AGI | Reflexes, movement, precision, initiative, balance correction |
| STA | Endurance, recovery, prolonged output, exhaustion resistance |
| WIS | Rimae sensitivity, control, perception, concentration |

On the default 60-point scale:

- Maximum HP = `3 × STR + 2 × AGI + 4 × STA`
- Maximum RP = `10 × WIS`
- Maximum Fatigue = `STR + 2 × AGI + 4 × STA`

There is no Charisma stat. Social outcomes depend on words, actions, evidence, context, Standing, timing, obligations, risk, and what other people want.

## Rimae and classes

Rimae is the world's magic and access to magical possibility. Every character has RP capacity, but **Spark** governs direct, freeform interaction with Rimae. Structured tools can provide indirect routes.

Classes are developmental directions, not permission gates or fixed builds:

- **Weaver** — develops direct Rimae practice through Spark.
- **Reader** — uses structured systems, runes, tools, preparations, and stored or routed RP.
- **Warrior** — develops physical capability, technique, defense, and martial expression.
- **Rogue** — develops access, movement, stealth, observation, and opportunistic technique.

Callings, professions, titles, combinations, and entirely different paths may emerge through play.

## How to play

Say what your character attempts in ordinary language. You may also speak out of character, ask for state, challenge accounting, change presentation, or request an audit. Backticks are a convenient System/OOC convention, not a requirement.

The DM resolves intent against capability, circumstances, uncertainty, Interpretation, Risk Mode, and the persistent world. Creativity can earn possibility or advantage; it does not guarantee success or override another character's agency.

Every ordinary response contains:

1. **Setting** — authoritative opening time, environment, and location.
2. **Narrative** — what occurs and what can legitimately be perceived.
3. **Status** — current player state and authoritative ending time.

Suggested Actions are optional and non-exhaustive. You may choose, combine, modify, or ignore them.

## Progress and continuity

EXP is awarded for meaningful, non-overlapping blocks of learning or exertion. The threshold to leave Level `L` is `100 + 10 × (L − 1)`. Each level grants two player-controlled stat points and one immediate DM-assigned Earned Point supported by demonstrated growth.

Material results persist. Work, injuries, Gold, inventory, custody, relationships, commitments, evidence, and unresolved Threads are not reset merely because a session or model changes.

If something looks wrong, ask plainly—for example, `I think my Gold is wrong`. Verified corrections repair canonical state without punishing the player or creating a competing timeline.

## The shortest version

> Act freely. The world responds coherently. Consequences persist. You may inspect or correct the state. No menu owns the story.
